## Editorial changes

- A: distinguish base form from third-person singular, including -s/-es/-ies and goes/does/has; distinguish ordinary verbs from be/modals. Habits, current ordinary states, facts and timetable statements do not prove an action is happening now or that a scheduled event actually happened.
- B: preserve the subject/action/time/place when changing polarity; do/does + not + base form, full and contracted variants. Not liking is not automatically hating; not working here does not describe every other place. Be/can/have got keep their own patterns.
- C: neutral direct yes/no and wh-questions, do/does, base form, factual polarity of short answers, and the subject-who exception. The visible criterion distinguishes neutral question formation from possible conversational echo/confirmation orders.
- D: explicitly teach the requested neutral mid-position, position after ordinary be, and frequency expressions without treating every other grammatical position as wrong. Not always is not never; doesn't usually does not mean never or establish a precise percentage.
- E: be + -ing for a process now or an unfinished current-period activity, with possible pauses. Correct writing/sitting/lying and agreement; stative meanings and future arrangements are bounded links, not claims that all temporary states use this form.
- F: am/is/are + not + -ing, standard am not/I'm not, is not/isn't and are not/aren't. Do not add don't before the existing be; lexical doing can remain. A negative description does not establish the opposite activity or permanent unemployment.

All eighteen mistake items have visible owned notes plus paired wrong/correct English and Ukrainian fields. Translations of wrong examples convey intended meaning, not grammatical endorsement.

The finite point-detail quality decisions keep all explanations in visible basic: zero artificial short details were added. Existing component types, colors, typography, spacing, examples, view, formatter, validator and practice/grading engines are unchanged.

Editorial focus A/B/C/D/E/F is A1/A1/A1/A2/A1/A1. Display range is A1–A2; the independently captured actual SQL practice filter remains NULL. These are different facts, not a fabricated CEFR equality. This is not complete A1–C2 course certification or a claim that selecting a ready-made studies token proves independent spelling.
